# ROHENI / November portfolio update

## What changed

- AtIr is corrected to completed Collection 001 work; it precedes Adwoa in both the homepage and collections index. Its case study uses all four supplied photographs/composites. AtIr is removed from the Collection 002 development gallery.
- Every Collection 001 project has fuller design and material stories. The About and Inspiration narratives now explain Kenyan making, African artistry and specific design decisions.
- Collection 002 has dedicated hoodie and premium recycled T-shirt directions, three supplied hoodie images, the T-shirt mockup and a filterable development archive. Final recycled fibre composition/percentage is not invented.
- The homepage Gikomba display now uses the supplied wide waste-site photograph, without desaturation. The supplied river image appears within the impact investigation, not as the unchanged display photo.
- `impact.html` is linked from navigation and homepage; `mission.html` remains a working alias.
- The impact page uses a zero-baseline chart, exact expandable table and downloadable CSV from Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, Economic Survey 2026, Table 6.6 (printed p.133). 2025 is provisional. Values: 2021 183830.1; 2022 177664.4; 2023 198083.5; 2024 230535.3; 2025 250527.3 tonnes. These are national imports, not Nairobi waste or ROHENI savings.
- Changing Markets’ February 2023 interview-based unsellable-bale range is labelled separately and never multiplied by later import tonnage.
